# Weekly Update: Restroom Ready (Five Girls), November 20, 2026

#### Accountability: what we did

- **Build:** Restored guest browsing on the [website](https://nyc.imfinethanku.me/find/) on November 19 so people can try a restroom lookup before creating an account. Saving places and contributing still require sign-in and agreement acceptance. We have not uploaded a new TestFlight build, so the installed beta is unchanged. Our App Store publication is still under review. We prioritized reducing the signup barrier over adding more features.
- **Grow:** Ran two Reddit Ads campaigns on November 15–17 and November 17–19. They did not go well because the audience was poorly matched to our NYC-focused product. The first campaign’s report included India (26.8%), the Philippines (11.1%) and Brazil (5.13%), showing substantial delivery outside our intended NYC audience. Both campaigns were shorter than the assigned seven-day experiment, and we are still collecting the performance totals.
- **Analyze:** Checked the [Google Analytics count API](https://nyc.imfinethanku.me/api/user-count) on November 19 at 8:15 PM EST. For October 10–November 19, it reported **55 signup users and 43 users who signed up and opened a restroom card**, a **78.2% completion rate**, with 36 iOS and 7 web completions. The November 13 snapshot was 43 signup users and 34 completions. This week’s measured user growth includes both website and iPhone users.
- **Research:** Renamed the usability folder from `hw8-usability-testing` to `hw7-usability-testing` and added the session responsibilities: Zheli Chen as moderator and Jiaqi as note-taker.

#### Planning: what we're doing next

- **Build:** Retest filter recovery, missing-information labels and the contribution-to-display workflow. Check that people can keep their selected restroom when they choose to sign in and save it. These were concrete points of friction in the sessions.
- **Grow:** Start the official seven-day ad campaign next week. Investigate the permanent Reddit Ads ban and explore other paid campaign channels. Continue neighborhood-group outreach with tagged links, and compare channels by how many relevant NYC users they bring rather than reach alone.
- **Analyze:** Recover impressions, clicks, spend, budgets and campaign-attributed signups, then compare results with the original success bar if it can be recovered. Check the new guest website flow separately from the signup-to-card-open funnel.
- **Research:** Run follow-up sessions with first-time users outside the team. Watch whether they find a useful restroom, understand unknown information and submit an update without help. Check when they see a reason to create an account.

#### Issues

- Reddit permanently banned our advertising account for an unknown reason. We are investigating the cause and looking for other campaign channels.
- Requiring signup before a first lookup was a barrier. The website now allows browsing first, while the installed iPhone beta still uses the earlier flow. We need follow-up evidence that guest access helps people reach a useful restroom and gives them a reason to save or contribute.
