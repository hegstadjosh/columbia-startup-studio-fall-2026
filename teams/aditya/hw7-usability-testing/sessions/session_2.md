## What we saw across the session

- **Hesitations:** Didn't know to filter using the chat on the left, so filtered visually instead.
- **Wrong clicks:** Tried to log in before signing up. Started in Favorites instead of Explore.
- **Questions out loud:** None recorded.
- **Dropped tasks:** None.
- **Workarounds:** Filtered by eye instead of using the chat search.
- **Reached the aha moment?** Yes (a short list of sublets that fit), though not through the chat search.

## Post-session answers

1. **When did you first understand what this is for?** Right away: the home page shows images of apartments with prices and addresses.
2. **What was the most confusing part?** Nothing except signing in.
3. **What would have made it easier?** Couldn't think of anything.
4. **Would you use this for a real search?** Yes. It showed apartments at very good prices, though the selection was very limited.

## Quick insight

The biggest thing this session showed us was that Messages is unclear (it looks like WhatsApp), and that we should present messaging as a core part of the product.
