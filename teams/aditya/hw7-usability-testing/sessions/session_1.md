## Task-by-task observations

### Task 1: First impression
"Without clicking anything yet, what do you think this product does? Who is it for?"

- **What they did:** Looked at the home page, noticed the buttons and the prices.
- **What they said out loud:** It helps find an apartment near Columbia. It's an LLM: she can type and it gives apartments.
- **Completed?** Yes
- **Moderator note:** Sam may have given away what the product is for before she answered.

### Task 2: Create an account
"Please try to create an account."

- **What they did:** Clicked "Sign in" first. Typed in her details and it didn't work. Then realized she wasn't signed up, went to sign up, and it worked.
- **What they said out loud:** Asked if it works with a Columbia email.
- **Completed?** With trouble

### Task 3: Find a place (aha moment)
"Imagine you have a summer internship in New York from June to August, and your budget is about $1,600 a month. Use Gable to find a place you'd actually consider."

- **What they did:** Started typing into the chat search right away, then clicked search. Clicked "Summer" in the header and ended up on a landing page instead of searching.
- **What they said out loud:** "wowwwww this is great!!!!" She said the chat search and the UI were clean. She didn't understand the "Replies in ~3h" line, or the "18 mins" and "Columbus Circle" lines on the listing cards.
- **Completed?** Yes

### Task 4: Save and compare
"Save two or three places you like, then compare them."

- **What they did:** Saw one place in each area and expected more options. Saved two. Opened the map.
- **What they said out loud:** Didn't understand why a card says "Columbus Circle" (then: "ohhh" once she got it). Said the map, the price and the photos were nice to have. Didn't understand that the listings come from Columbia students.
- **Completed?** Yes

## What we saw across the session

- **Hesitations:** The "Replies in ~3h" line and the Columbus Circle / Times Square lines on listing cards.
- **Wrong clicks:** "Sign in" before creating an account. "Summer" in the header, which left the search.
- **Questions out loud:** "Does a Columbia email work?" Why a card says "Columbus Circle."
- **Dropped tasks:** None.
- **Workarounds:** None.
- **Reached the aha moment?** Yes, right away when she typed her request into the chat search.

## Post-session answers

1. **When did you first understand what this is for?** Right away, when she opened the site.
2. **What was the most confusing part?** Why a card says "20 minutes to Times Square" and "15 minutes to Columbus Circle." It didn't make sense to her.
3. **What would have made it easier?** Pretty easy, nothing too confusing. It reminded her of Airbnb.

## Quick insight

The biggest thing this session showed us was that the chat search is the moment it clicks, but the text on each listing card confused her, and clicking "Summer" took her out of the search.
