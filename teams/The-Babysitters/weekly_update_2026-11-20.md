# The Babysitters — Weekly update, November 20

## Accountability

- **Build.** Last week we planned to make the challenge-to-skill flow work end to end and to simplify the visit experience into one page. We released the challenge flow (entries, voting, results, credits and grading scripts) and a new homepage on Nov 18, and one practice challenge is open. We did not simplify the visit page; the challenge release took the week.

- **Grow.** We planned to invite the people we interviewed to try the completed flow. We have not invited them yet, because the flow went live only on Nov 18. We launched a Reddit ad on Nov 18, later than the course asked and without committing an experiment card first. After one day it had 865 impressions, 7 clicks and $5.23 spent. Our public count is 20 sign-ups.

- **Analyze.** We read the first day of ad numbers and saw the campaign is spending slowly. We also found that our public count mixes sign-ups and sign-ins, so it does not tell us how many people completed the core action.

- **Research.** We planned to test the completed flow with strangers and did not. We reviewed how our unattended visits run and found that they remove the owner's instructions and skills for safety, so a visit's result reflects the model more than the owner's setup. In one small experiment, 18 runs of the same brief, results differed by model and by a task-relevant skill.

## Planning

- **Build.** One results page per entry that shows what the agent did, its status and its result, because both testers could not tell what was happening. Publish the first winning skill to skills.sh.

- **Grow.** Seed the open challenge with our own entries so the first visitor does not see an empty board, then invite our testers, classmates and r/ClaudeCode with tagged links. These are the groups where people already run coding agents.

- **Analyze.** Count entries, returns for results and votes per channel. Read the Reddit ad once at its seven-day end against the experiment card. Change the public count to report the core action.

- **Research.** Usability sessions on the challenge flow with people outside the team, including Jonah and Collin, to see whether they can enter without help.

## Issues

- Few people outside the team have used the product on their own.
- The ad launched without a committed experiment card and is spending slowly.
- Our public user count does not yet report the core action.
