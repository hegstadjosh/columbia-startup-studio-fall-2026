# Part 3 — Backlog, ranked

**Inputs.** Two hands-on usability sessions (Jonah and Collin, both Nov 12) and one concept conversation (Mansi's SIPA participant, Nov 10). One tester found the order of sign-in, install and pairing confusing. Both testers struggled to tell what the agent was doing and when results were ready. One found dollar estimates confusing next to a subscription. The concept participant wanted reusable skills for her own work. From our own data: the challenge flow went live on Nov 18 and has no outside entries yet.

Score = reach × impact × confidence ÷ effort. The numbers are our estimates.

| Rank | Item | Type | Why | R / I / C / E | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | Get the first outside entries into the live challenge: seed it with our own entries, then invite named people | Growth | Nothing else can be judged until someone outside the team uses the flow | 8 / 3 / 50% / 2 | 6.0 |
| 2 | Count users by the core action (an entry or a completed visit), not sign-ins | Infra | Our public count mixes sign-ups and sign-ins | 3 / 2 / 100% / 1 | 6.0 |
| 3 | One results page per entry: what the agent did, its status, its result, what to do next | Feature | Both testers could not tell what was happening or when results were ready | 8 / 2 / 80% / 3 | 4.3 |
| 4 | Publish each winning skill to skills.sh and track installs | Feature | Gives people without an agent in the contest a reason to come, and gives us a public number | 8 / 2 / 50% / 2 | 4.0 |
| 5 | Show usage per run in plain terms instead of dollar estimates | Quick win | One tester found dollar figures confusing next to a subscription | 5 / 1 / 80% / 1 | 4.0 |
| 6 | Decide how much of the owner's own setup an unattended visit may use, and say so plainly | Product | Unattended visits remove the owner's instructions and skills for safety, so results reflect the model more than the owner | 8 / 3 / 50% / 5 | 2.4 |
| 7 | Automatic checks for challenge entries (a teammate runs them by hand today) | Infra | Needed once entries arrive | 3 / 1 / 50% / 3 | 0.5 |

**Why item 1 is starred.** Items 1 and 2 tie on score. Item 2 is about an hour of work and we do it regardless. Item 1 decides whether the reach and confidence we gave every other item are real. The rest are in score order; ties go to the item that helps a first-time entrant sooner.

**What we are not building now, and why.**

- New kinds of activity, until the live challenge has outside entries. We do not yet know whether the one we shipped works.
- Agent social features. They need many agents present at once, which we do not have.
- Cash prizes. They raise contest-law questions we cannot settle this semester.
