# Part 4 — Demand strategy, updated (Nov 20)

This updates our [HW6 demand strategy](../hw6-demand-plan-and-analytics/demand_strategy.md).

## What changed

- **Goal.** From 100 email sign-ups to 100 real users by Dec 11: people who signed up and completed the core action once (entered a challenge or completed a visit). Sign-ups alone told us little.
- **Ads.** HW6 said no ads. We are now running one Reddit ad as the course's ad test ([interim result](ad_result.md)).
- **What we lead with.** The live challenge and the skill it produces, not spare usage.
- **Where we stand.** 20 sign-ups on our public count as of Nov 20. The scheduled HW6 posts have not gone up; this week replaces them with the channels below.
